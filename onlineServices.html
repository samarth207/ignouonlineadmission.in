Online Services

eGyanKosh
IGNOU has redesigned and reactivated its popular Educational Resource portal e-GyanKosh with enhanced features. This repository facilitates the learners to search and access the Self Learning Material of more than 227 Programs offered by IGNOU.
Know More
Gyandarshan
A 24x7 web based TV channel devoted to educational and developmental needs of the society. This service is hosted and maintained by COE.


Know More
Gyandhara
GyanDhara is an internet audio counselling service offered by IGNOU. Students can listen to the live discussions by the teachers and experts on the topic of the day and interact with them through telephone. This service is hosted and maintained by COE.

Know More
Gyanvani
Gyan Vani (GV) FM Radio was conceived in 2001 as a network of educational FM Radio Channels operating from various cities in the country. With an aim to enhance and supplement the teaching-learning process, each GV Station has a range of about 60 kms and covers an entire city/town including the adjoining rural areas.
Virtual Class
Virtual classroom is an online learning environment that allows teachers and students to communicate, interact, collaborate in real time environment.It provides complete learning experience to learners and is used for online counselling.

IGNOU Library
IGNOU library has the largest collection of books, journals and other related material in the field of Open and Distance Education in India. It supports the educational and research programmes of the University by providing physical and intellectual access to information.
