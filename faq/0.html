You can register on the Student Portal of the University and create your student account in a few simple steps:
  1. visit https://ignou.samarth.edu.in
  2. Please click NEW REGISTRATION
  3. Provide the Program, Name and Enrolment Number and submit
  4. A One-time Password (OTP) shall be sent to your registered email ID. Please enter the OTP on the next screen and follow the instructions. Please remember the password you have selected.
  5. Once your registration is successful, return to https://ignou.samarth.edu.in and login. Your Enrolment Number is your Username.
  6. You will find your registration details in your login. Click ‘Services’ tab on the left side of the page.
  7. You will find a host of services available here. The option to download your student identity card is also available.
  8. When you click ‘Download I-Card’, a PDF file shall be downloaded. Please use your Enrolment Number to open the file.

To Reset the lost password:
  • Using the Lost password option you can rest your password.
  • Click on Lost password? Link availble in login page or visit directly to: https://lms-evbab.ignouonlineadmission.in/login/forgot_password.php
  • Under the section “Search by email address” Enter your registered email id and click on search. Email id should be same as provided in admission form.
  • Then click continue.
  • You will receive a token link in your email ID which will be valid for 30 Minutes only.
  • Click on the link and set you new password.
