1 Admission Portal for Online Programmes January 2026 Session (for Indian students)

Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) has commenced the January 2026 Admission Cycle for programmes offered through Online Mode.

Key Dates:

  • Last Date for Fresh Admissions: 31st January 2026

How to Apply:
  • For Online Mode Programmes: Apply via: https://ignouiop.samarth.edu.in/

Steps for Fresh Applicants:
  • Create a new registration on the respective portal.
  • Submit all required details carefully.
  • Select the desired programme.
  • Read and follow the instructions while filling out the application form.

Programmes Offered: IGNOU offers a wide range of programmes, including:
  • Master's Degrees
  • Bachelor's Degrees
  • PG Diploma & Diploma
  • PG Certificate & Certificate Programmes
  • Appreciation/Awareness Level Programmes

  • Online Mode: Programme Details - Online
