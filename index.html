HIGH QUALITY EDUCATION

In the context of providing high quality education, IGNOU has approached the task at two levels: i) as an institution, and ii) as a system-leader. As an institution the University is committed to quality and excellence in all its activities. As a system leader, the University envisages a proactive role for itself by sharing professional capabilities and resources, to maintain and coordinate standards of Distance Education in the country. As a system leader in ODL, the University is also undergoing the process of NAAC Accreditation.
